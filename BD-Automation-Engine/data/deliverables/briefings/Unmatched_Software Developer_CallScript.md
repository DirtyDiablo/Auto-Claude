# Call Script: Software Developer - Unmatched

## Opening (30 seconds)
"Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] from [Company]. I'm reaching out about your Software Developer position for the Unmatched program. Is this a good time for a quick 5-minute call?"

## Hook (1 minute)
"I've been tracking the Unmatched program and noticed you have some critical staffing needs, particularly for Secret-cleared talent in Unknown Location. We've had success helping similar programs fill these hard-to-find positions."

## Discovery Questions
1. "What's your current timeline for filling this Software Developer role?"
2. "Are you working with GDIT directly or through a sub?"
3. "What are the biggest challenges you're facing with Unmatched staffing?"
4. "Is the Secret clearance requirement firm, or is there flexibility?"

## Value Proposition (2 minutes)
"Our team has successfully supported multiple DoD/IC programs with similar requirements. We maintain an active pipeline of Secret-cleared professionals and can typically present qualified candidates within 2 weeks."

## Key Talking Points
- Our Secret cleared candidate pipeline
- Past performance on DoD/IC programs
- Understanding of Unmatched requirements
- Rapid deployment capability

## Pain Point Questions
- "What's been the biggest challenge filling Secret positions?"
- "Are you seeing any changes in program scope or budget?"
- "How is your current teaming arrangement working?"

## Close
"Based on what you've shared, I think we could be a strong fit for your Unmatched needs. Can we schedule a follow-up call to discuss specific candidates/capabilities?"

## Objection Handling
- "We're already working with vendors" → "Understood. Many of our clients use us as a complementary source, especially for hard-to-fill cleared positions. Would it be helpful to have a backup option?"
- "No budget" → "I hear that. When do you anticipate your next funding cycle? I'd like to stay in touch for when needs arise."
- "Send me information" → "Happy to. What specific capabilities are most relevant to your current priorities?"
