
================================================================================
BD CALL SCRIPT TEMPLATE
================================================================================

Use the following template fields from the CSV (columns at the end of each row):
- {FIRST_NAME} - Contact's first name
- {COMPANY} - Contact's company (e.g., GDIT)
- {JOB_TITLE_SHORT} - Abbreviated job title
- {LOCATION_SHORT} - City/location
- {CLEARANCE_LEVEL} - Security clearance level
- {PROGRAM_NAME} - Associated program name
- {ROLE_TYPE} - Type of role (Engineering, Analysis, etc.)

================================================================================
OPENING SCRIPT
================================================================================

"Hi {FIRST_NAME}, this is [YOUR NAME] with Prime Technical Services.

I'm reaching out because we're currently supporting some {ROLE_TYPE} positions
in {LOCATION_SHORT} and your background at {COMPANY} caught my attention.

Do you have a quick moment?"

[IF YES, CONTINUE. IF NO, ASK FOR BETTER TIME]

================================================================================
VALUE PROPOSITION
================================================================================

"We're working with a client on their {PROGRAM_NAME} program who needs
{JOB_TITLE_SHORT} talent with {CLEARANCE_LEVEL} clearance.

Based on your experience at {COMPANY}, you seem like you could be a great fit -
or might know someone who would be interested.

The role is in {LOCATION_SHORT} and offers competitive compensation."

================================================================================
QUALIFYING QUESTIONS
================================================================================

1. "Are you currently happy in your role at {COMPANY}, or are you open to
   exploring new opportunities?"

2. "Is your {CLEARANCE_LEVEL} clearance still active?"

3. "Would the {LOCATION_SHORT} area work for you, or are you looking for
   something closer to home?"

4. "What's most important to you in your next role - compensation, growth
   opportunities, or the type of work?"

================================================================================
CLOSE / NEXT STEPS
================================================================================

[IF INTERESTED]
"Great! I'd love to tell you more about this opportunity. Can I send you the
full job description? What's the best email to reach you at?"

[IF NOT INTERESTED BUT REFERRAL POSSIBLE]
"I understand. Would you happen to know anyone in your network who might be
interested in {ROLE_TYPE} opportunities in {LOCATION_SHORT}?"

[IF NOT INTERESTED]
"No problem at all, {FIRST_NAME}. I appreciate your time. Would it be okay if
I kept you in mind for future opportunities that might be a better fit?"

================================================================================
