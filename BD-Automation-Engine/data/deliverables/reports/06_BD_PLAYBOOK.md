## Conversation Scripts

### Contractor Introduction Call
> "Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] with [Company]. I'm reaching out because I'll be taking over as your account manager going forward. Colton has moved on, and I wanted to introduce myself and make sure you have everything you need. How is the assignment going?"

### Client Introduction Email
> Subject: Account Transition - [Company Name]
>
> Dear [Client Name],
>
> I wanted to reach out to introduce myself. I'll be taking over management of our relationship with [Prime Contractor] effective immediately.
>
> I've reviewed our partnership history and am impressed by the success we've had together, including [X] placements on [Program]. My priority is ensuring continuity and continuing to deliver top talent for your programs.
>
> I'd welcome the opportunity to schedule a brief call to introduce myself and discuss your 2026 hiring plans.
>
> Best regards,
> [Your Name]

### Reactivation Call (Cold Contacts)
> "Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] with [Company]. I'm following up on a conversation you had with my colleague Colton last [timeframe]. I understand you were discussing [topic/program]. I'm now handling this account and wanted to see if there are any current or upcoming needs I can help with."
