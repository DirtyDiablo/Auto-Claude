## OBJECTION HANDLING

### "We're not hiring right now"
> "Completely understand. When hiring does pick up, what roles typically open first? I'd like to have candidates ready so we can move quickly when the time comes."

### "We use other staffing firms"
> "That makes sense - many of our clients work with multiple partners. We've found success filling the harder-to-find cleared technical roles. When you have one of those challenging reqs, I'd love the chance to show what we can do."

### "Send me your rates"
> "Happy to. To make sure I quote competitively, what clearance levels and skills are you typically hiring? That way I can send relevant rates rather than a generic sheet."

### "I'll reach out when we have openings"
> "Appreciate that. In the meantime, would it be helpful if I sent you a monthly brief on market conditions for [skill/clearance] talent in [location]? That way you'll have insight into availability when needs arise."
